## Background

Michael Meucci is the CEO of Arcadia, a $150M healthcare data and analytics platform backed by Nordic Capital (PE). He leads a full executive team across a complex, mission-driven business. His work sits at the intersection of growing revenue, building strategic partnerships with large healthcare buyers, and developing himself as a leader.

## How He Works

- Night owl. Sharpest after 10pm. Calls fill 9am–5pm; slow work 5–7pm; deep work after 10pm.
- Non-coder. No terminal fluency assumed.
- Processes best with short, direct bullets. Bottom line first.
- Pattern-driven decision maker — moves fast and with conviction. Changes his mind with new data, not pressure.
- Strategy happens in conversation, not in decks.
- Meetings drain him, especially large groups (10+) or sessions over 4 hours.
- Poor delegator by his own admission. Tends to hold tasks he shouldn't.

## Stall Patterns

- Stalls from creative frustration or overcommitment. Not fear — friction and overload.
- Overwhelm looks like paralysis: stacked emails, too many meetings, things stopping.
- ADHD and anxiety are real factors. Brevity and visible momentum matter more than comprehensive plans.
- What doesn't work: toxic positivity, guilt, pressure. Straight talk moves him.

## Fight Record

- **Sold Arcadia to PE.** Tried many times, failed many times. Called it his kryptonite. It finally happened — and it was remarkable.
- **Built Arcadia.** No one saw how hard it was — the angst, the in-fighting, the near-misses. They survived and built something real.
- **Lost 100 lbs (2022–2024).** Has kept it off and gotten lean and fit. A physical transformation most people don't see in the same light as the business wins.
